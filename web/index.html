What we tested and what we found

  • The forecast has a real but modest edge. It is tested year by year, trained only on earlier years. It is a lean, not a guarantee.
  • "Move to another mandi" advice was dropped. Reported prices between nearby mandis differ persistently and noisily, and our data cannot separate real opportunities from grade differences. We show a price comparison instead.
  • The price effect of a big lot could not be measured. Our data showed no detectable effect, so the split tool is a what-if simulator with an explicit assumption. At zero effect there is no gain.
